## 3. Technical Learnings
- **The schema signal finds join keys, not topics.** Without location-only columns it matches on neighbourhood, ward, address and zoning. Fire Response → drainage assets was caused by `latitude`/`longitude`/`geometry_point` before the fix.
- **`:@computed_region_<xxxx>_<xxxx>`** columns: 977 of them on 174 real datasets, added by Socrata (point-in-polygon against a boundary dataset). They're not publisher content. Recorded in `data/DATA.md`.
- **Field names are sparse:** 8715 of 10576 distinct names occur once.
- **Programme boilerplate dominates both signals for Insight Community surveys.** Graffiti's neighbours are other surveys, matched by text wording and by the shared `q*_study_profiling_questionnaire_2014` columns. Expect low precision on survey cards.
- **Purpose field** (`General-Information_Purpose`): filled on 790 of 1421, with 209 distinct texts.
  - 561 are exact copies of boilerplate: Speed Check Sign 264, census ~170, monthly survey 88.
  - 229 are specific (used on 3 or fewer datasets), with a median of 5 new words beyond name, description and tags.
  - Not used anywhere yet; queued as a variant.
- **Usage fields are unused:** `page_views.*` and `download_count` are in every Discovery record and nothing reads them yet. They would be a cheap M2 addition.
- **Bootstrap gotchas:**
  - Resampling with replacement inflates ARI and NMI, because duplicates agree with themselves.
  - NMI also rises as n falls at many clusters.
  - AMI on 80% subsets is biased downward.
  - Only ARI's subset interval is trustworthy here.
