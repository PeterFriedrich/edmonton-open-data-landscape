  1. **`src/layout.py`**
     - Distance = 1 − mean(text, schema).
     - Normal week: warm t-SNE (`early_exaggeration=1`, `max_iter=250`, init from last week; new units start at the median of their 5 nearest neighbours), then Procrustes onto last week's map.
     - A new topic (a new unit's top-10 neighbours mostly other new units): `max_iter=1000`, and the week is flagged for the change log.
     - First week or re-anchor: `init="random"` with a fixed seed and an epoch bump. Re-anchor at cumulative drift over ~10% median (a guess) or quarterly.
     - Output: `coords.csv` (unit, x, y, epoch, is_new) plus a summary (median/p95 movement, mode, flags). Removed units are listed, not dropped silently.
     - Tests: a displacement gate (median ≤ 2.5%, p95 ≤ 5% unless flagged), new-unit placement, and reported removals.
  2. **`src/export.py`** writes to `site/data/`:
     - `search.json`;
     - `units/<id>.json` (link out, top-10 neighbours, join partners labelled "shares a column name", theme, claimed vs actual update rate, series members);

     Fields come from an allow-list, with a test that no description text gets out. Theme labels without `reviewed=true` publish as "Theme N". The claimed-vs-actual update-rate logic must move out of `notebooks/m2_metrics.ipynb` into `src/`.
