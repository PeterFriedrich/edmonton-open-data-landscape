## 3. Technical Learnings
- **Layout stability.** Measured on the 2026-10-02 matrices, with D = 1 − mean(text, schema) and 920 units. Full numbers in `TODO.md` M4; script in the research folder above.
  - "Warm" means sklearn `TSNE(metric="precomputed", init=<last week's coordinates>, early_exaggeration=1, max_iter=250)`, with new units placed at the median of their 5 nearest neighbours.
  - With 15 random arrivals, the warm run moves the map a median 1.0–2.0% (p95 ≤ 3.3%), against 5.5–9.2% for a fresh run, with the same recall (0.53).
  - Over 8 chained weeks, drift adds up to a 6.5% median (p95 13%), and recall stays equal to a fresh run.
  - **A whole new cluster arriving at once separates poorly with the warm run.** Its share of 2D neighbours in its own cluster is 0.36–0.76, against 0.66–0.94 fresh. `max_iter=1000` restores 0.56–0.85 but moves the map 5–6%.
  - The sklearn default `early_exaggeration=12` cancels out the init. `init="pca"` is rejected with `metric="precomputed"`.
- **sigma.js:** 4.0.0 (2026-10-08) is a complete rewrite: a new styles and primitives API, WebGL 2 only. 4.0.1 is 391 KB against 188 KB for 3.0.3. Both are UMDs exposing `Sigma`. Start on 3.0.3.
- **Observable Plot** 0.6.17's UMD reads a global `d3`, so vendor d3 7 (`d3.min.js`, 279,706 bytes) as well.
- **The name is taken:** `opendataedmonton.ca` ("Open Data Edmonton") is live, uses the heading "Edmonton Open Data Atlas", and has 18 datasets in 6 themes. Avoid both names.
